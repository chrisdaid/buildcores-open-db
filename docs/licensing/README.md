# OpenDB Licensing Guide

OpenDB uses unmodified [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](../../LICENSE.txt) for identified new protected database material. [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md) defines coverage and attribution. **No new material is currently designated; existing ODC-By permissions, including commercial use, remain available.**

## Community use and forks

Covered material may be copied, adapted, and shared for NonCommercial purposes under the license. Retain attribution and notices, identify changes, and include the license text or link. Publicly shared protected adaptations must use an adapter's license allowed by CC section 3(b). Private changes need not be published, and independent application code is not automatically covered.

NonCommercial has the purpose-based meaning in CC section 1(k). Nonprofit status, free access, donations, grants, or paid staff do not alone decide whether a use qualifies. Describe advertising, affiliate revenue, paid access, or other business purposes when seeking permission. These examples add no restrictions to the standard license.

Forks retain the original covered material's noncommercial terms. BuildCores does not acquire rights over outside-fork additions; commercial use of a whole modified fork may require permission from its authors as well. A license cannot create exclusive rights in ordinary product facts.

## Commercial requests

Start a [licensing inquiry](https://github.com/buildcores/buildcores-open-db/issues/new) with the intended product, data release, monetization, and any raw-data, API, or fork redistribution. Keep confidential details out of public issues and arrange private follow-up with the maintainers.

Permission requires an express written grant from an authorized rights holder. The [commercial agreement](COMMERCIAL_AGREEMENT.md) specifies material, use, recipients, term, fees, and downstream rights. Its default public-sharing duties retain attribution and ShareAlike. Approval alone does not waive those duties or approve recipients' commercial use; distribution, commercial sublicensing, and waivers need explicit scope.

The [contributor agreement](CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md) and commercial agreement are unsigned templates. Complete the legal parties, contacts, and applicable terms before acceptance.

## Maintainer review

Before accepting newly covered protected material:

1. Verify sources, the rights holder, applicable permissions, and legacy portions. Obtain an express contributor grant when BuildCores needs commercial sublicensing rights; a PR checkbox is not acceptance.
2. Keep the exact agreement version and acceptance privately. Add the material, revision, attribution, provenance, and licensing authority to `NOTICE.md` before release.
3. Include `LICENSE.txt`, `NOTICE.md`, and applicable legacy and third-party notices in releases, exports, and mirrors. Verify their scope in the delivered files.

This is a manual review process. Hold protected contributions needing a commercial grant until authority is verified. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#licensing-and-contributor-permissions).

The [preserved ODC-By license](ODC-By-1.0.txt) records legacy terms. [Archived research and custom drafts](archive/RESEARCH.md) explain the alternatives; they add no conditions to the selected license.
