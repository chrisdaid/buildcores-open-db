## 1 Contributions covered

“Contribution” means material You intentionally submit for inclusion in BuildCores OpenDB after accepting this agreement, and any earlier contributions specifically listed in a signed addendum. It includes data, original database selection or arrangement, annotations, and documentation identified in the submission.

Merely maintaining a fork, filing a question, or linking to third-party material is not a Contribution. Material marked “not a contribution” is excluded. A reference to someone else's source does not make that source Your original work.

“You” means the rights holder signing or accepting this agreement. Someone accepting for an organization must have authority to bind that organization. An employee cannot license employer-owned material without the necessary permission.

BuildCores agrees that a Contribution accepted into a public OpenDB release will be made available under the adopted BuildCores OpenDB Community License 1.0 or another public license granting at least those noncommercial permissions. Commercial licensing may be offered in parallel. Existing grants to community recipients will not be withdrawn merely because BuildCores offers a commercial license.

BuildCores may decline, correct, or remove a Contribution, including to address rights, privacy, or accuracy concerns. It need not publish a rejected submission or continue distributing a particular release. These choices do not withdraw permissions already validly granted.

You confirm that You have authority to grant the rights above. For any material not originally created by You, identify its source, rights holder where known, applicable license, and relevant restrictions. Disclose employer, client, confidentiality, privacy, or contractual restrictions of which You are aware.

Do not submit copied manuals, images, proprietary databases, confidential information, or personal information without appropriate permission. Public availability of a source is not, by itself, permission to copy its protected content. A citation is provenance evidence, not a substitute for permission.

Ordinary facts may not carry copyright; this agreement grants only rights that actually exist and that You can grant. It does not certify that every fact You submit creates an exclusive right for BuildCores.

This agreement applies only after express acceptance recorded against this exact version. A repository's general contribution instructions or an earlier pull request do not establish acceptance. Amendments require the contributor's express agreement; a later website edit cannot expand an existing grant.

The parties may use an electronic signature or another process that identifies the accepting rights holder and clearly records agreement. Keep legal names and private contact information outside public pull requests.
