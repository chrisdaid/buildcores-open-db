Distribution and sublicensing are permitted only to the extent specified in the order and section 3.

This agreement independently authorizes only the Commercial Use identified in the order. For approved uses under this commercial grant, Customer must comply with sections 5 and 6 of the adopted Community License identified in the order as conditions of this agreement, except for specific obligations expressly waived in that order. This includes reciprocal licensing of Customer-controlled protected database changes when shared with an independent recipient, whether publicly or privately. Processing solely on Customer's behalf under section 3 does not trigger the section 5 shared-copy obligations or require publication of Customer's unshared changes; supplied notices must still be preserved.

A waiver must identify the affected obligations, rights, distributions, and recipients. Commercial permission does not by itself waive the shared-copy, attribution, or notice duties. Neither permission nor a waiver transfers Customer's commercial approval to recipients, supplies another rights holder's permission, or withdraws any independently available public or legacy license. Customer may keep its approved internal modifications private and may use them in the approved product. Customer owns rights it creates, subject to BuildCores' retained rights in underlying material. No contribution of Customer's changes to BuildCores is required.

BuildCores retains ownership of its material. This agreement does not claim exclusive rights in unprotected facts and does not restrict Customer's independently obtained information, statutory exceptions, or permissions it already holds under ODC-By or another license. Customer may rely on those independent permissions according to their terms.

That handling does not itself offer public rights in Customer's unshared changes. Permission to display data in an approved product does not automatically permit sale of raw database copies, an independent data API, sublicensing to another business, or distribution of a commercially reusable fork. For authorized sharing with an independent recipient, unless the order expressly provides different downstream rights and identifies any relevant waivers under section 2, Customer must provide the adopted Community License and required notices, preserve the applicable direct public offers for BuildCores-controlled material, and offer Customer-controlled protected database changes under that same License. ## 5 Payment term and termination

Customer will pay the amounts at the times specified in the order. Unless that order expressly says otherwise, renewal requires a new agreement; there is no automatic renewal or right to future releases.

Either party may terminate for a material breach remaining uncured 30 days after written notice describing the breach. BuildCores may suspend an affected commercial permission while an unauthorized redistribution or sublicensing continues. Restoration or settlement of earlier unauthorized use must be expressly agreed; signing this agreement does not retroactively approve it.

At expiration or termination, Customer must stop uses requiring the expired commercial grant and remove affected copies from active commercial systems within 30 days. It may retain legally required records and inaccessible archival backups until ordinary deletion, without active use. Continuing end-user licenses, distributed products, model artifacts, and wind-down rights require explicit treatment in the order; none is assumed.

Termination does not revoke independently valid legacy licenses, a compliant public noncommercial grant, or surviving downstream rights expressly authorized by the order. Outstanding payment obligations and provisions needed to enforce accrued rights survive.
